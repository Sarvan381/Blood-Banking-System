AABB & WHO Medical Standards

Select a blood type below to analyze red blood cell (RBC) and blood plasma compatibility guidelines.

Universal Red Cell Donor (O Negative)

O- red blood cells lack A, B, and Rh antigens. In trauma emergencies (e.g. NH-48 or highway accidents) where there is no time to cross-match, O- is the gold standard for immediate transfusion. Only ~2% of the Indian population has O- blood, making it critical to reserve for STAT emergencies.

Universal Plasma Donor (AB Positive)

AB+ plasma has no anti-A or anti-B antibodies, making it universally compatible with patients of any blood group. In contrast, AB+ individuals can receive red blood cells from any group.
